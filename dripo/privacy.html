Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Android app "Dripo - Drip Coffee Timer & Guide" (the "App"), provided by nimrod project (the "Service Provider") as a free, ad-supported service. It explains what information the App handles and why, where and how long it is kept, and how you can manage and delete it.

Information stored on your device

Your brew history (date and time, recipe, servings, grind size, brew time, rating, notes and coffee beans), your selected coffee beans (including bean names you enter), the servings, grind size and recipe you last selected, your settings (sounds, voice guide and vibration), the number of brews you have completed, and whether the first-time guide has been shown are stored on your device. Unless you sign in, their content is not stored on the Service Provider's servers (for the usage statistics that are sent, see "Information collected by third-party services" below).

Of the App's data, only your selected coffee beans (including bean names you enter), the servings, grind size and recipe you last selected, your settings, the number of brews you have completed, and whether the first-time guide has been shown are included in Google's backup (Android Auto Backup); your brew history is not. On Android 12 or later, when you move data directly from an old device to a new one, your history is moved as well (on Android 11 or earlier, your history is not moved). Your sign-in state itself (your Firebase Authentication sign-in information) is not moved by either method. On Android 9 or later, while you are signed in, a separate restore key is created and used to sign you in automatically on a new device; on a device with a screen lock, the key is stored in Google's backup (see "Staying signed in after changing devices" below).

The App notifies you of brewing progress. Notifications are created on your device; no notification server is used.

Information handled when you sign in

Signing in is optional. Every brewing feature works without it. If you sign in with your Google account, your brew records are saved to the cloud so that you can see them after changing devices or on your other devices. Sign-in uses Google's Firebase Authentication.

  • Account information: your Google account's email address, name and profile photo URL (received from Google when you sign in), your user ID in the App, and when the account was created and last signed in. Used to identify your account and manage your sign-in.
  • Brew records: date and time, recipe, servings, grind size, brew time, rating, notes, coffee bean name, the type of device that wrote the record (Android or iOS), and when it was last updated. Records you saved on the device before signing in are added to your account when you sign in. Used to store your records in the cloud and sync them between your devices.
  • Staying signed in after changing devices: on Android 9 or later, while you are signed in, the App creates a restore key on your device so that you are signed in automatically on a new device (Android Restore Credentials). On Android 8, no restore key is created, so please sign in again on your new device. Android encrypts the key with your device's screen lock and stores it in Google's backup (on a device without a screen lock, it is kept only on the device). The Service Provider's server stores the public key used to verify it, the key's ID, your user ID, an identifier derived from the App's signing certificate, and when the key was created and used and how many times.

This information is stored in Google's cloud (Firebase, in data centers in the United States). It is encrypted in transit.

When you sign out, the account's records and the restore key are removed from your device. Your records stay in the cloud and come back when you sign in again.

Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following services provided by Google. These services may collect information automatically when you use the App.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows ads. It may collect your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and OS information, ad interaction data (such as ad views and taps), diagnostics and performance data, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. This information is used to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often the same ad is shown, and prevent fraud.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects how the App is used: the screens you view, actions such as starting and finishing a brew, the recipe, servings and grind size you choose, the rating you give, whether you entered a note, and sign-in, account deletion and restore key events and their results. It also collects the app instance ID, the advertising ID, your device model, OS and app versions, and your country or region (estimated from your IP address). The text of your notes and bean names, your email address and your user ID in the App are not sent. This information is used to understand and improve how the App is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (the App's state at the time of a crash, device model, OS and app versions, and the Crashlytics installation ID) and the type of errors that occur while syncing with the cloud. This information is used to investigate and fix problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config uses the Firebase installation ID to fetch the App's settings (such as whether to show ads).

The Service Provider does not sell your information and does not provide it to any third party other than the services listed above. The privacy policies of these services are:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, Remote Config)
Your choices
  • If you do not sign in, your brew records and account information are not stored on the Service Provider's servers.
  • To reset or delete your advertising ID (you can change this at any time), open Android Settings and go to Privacy > Ads (names vary by device).
  • To stop all collection, uninstall the App. If you were signed in, your records remain in the cloud; see "Deleting your data" below to remove them.
Retention
  • Information on your device: kept until you delete it (you can delete history entries one at a time on the App's history screen) or uninstall the App. Copies in Google's backup may remain after you uninstall the App and follow your Google backup settings.
  • Account information and cloud records: kept until you delete your account. When you delete a record, its content is also removed from the cloud, and only a deletion marker (the record's ID and timestamps) is kept so that your other devices learn of the deletion. These markers are also removed when you delete your account.
  • Restore keys: the key on your device is removed when you sign out (if it could not be removed, it is removed the next time you open the App). Keys on the Service Provider's server are removed when you delete your account. When you sign out, the App also asks the server to remove the key, but keys that could not be removed (for example, when you are offline) or that are no longer used (for example, after uninstalling the App) may remain until an account has more than 10 keys and the oldest ones are removed.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: kept according to the Google Analytics data retention setting, then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Google AdMob: kept according to Google's data retention policy.
Deleting your data

You can delete your account and the records saved in the cloud at any time in the App under Settings > Sign-in and account > Delete account. Your account information, cloud records and restore keys are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. The account's records on that device are also removed. Rarely, records sent immediately after deletion from another device that is still signed in, or restore key information that was being created, may remain. We recommend signing out on your other devices before deleting your account; if anything remains, contact us at the email address below and we will delete it.

If you have already uninstalled the App, you can request deletion as described on the account deletion request page.

The information collected by the third-party services above is not linked to your name or contact details, so the Service Provider usually cannot identify it. Information collected by Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics is deleted automatically after the retention periods above, and information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's data retention policy. For any other request about your data, please contact the email address below. We will reply within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take the necessary action.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect your information. Cloud records can be read and written only when signed in to the owner's account. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.
