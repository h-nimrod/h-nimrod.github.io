# nimrod project

Welcome to nimrod project. We develop mobile applications focused on finance, productivity, and everyday utilities.

---

## Apps

### Mortgage/Loan Calculator

Calculate your monthly payments, total interest, and compare loan scenarios with ease.

- Monthly payment estimation with principal/interest breakdown
- Total interest and payment calculation
- Loan comparison tool
- Available on **iOS** and **Android**

[Learn more](/loan/) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/mortgage-loan-calculator/id6739606505) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hnimrod.loancalc)

---

### Accumulate+

Compound interest calculator for regular investment planning. Visualize long-term growth of your investments.

- Compound interest simulation
- Withdrawal simulation for retirement planning
- Detailed annual projections with age and calendar year
- Available on **Android**

[Learn more](/accumulate_plus/) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hnimrod.accumulate_plus)

---

### Network Speed Test

Test your Internet connection speed easily. Automatically finds the best server and measures speed and ping latency.

- Download/Upload speed measurement
- Ping latency testing
- Visual chart display
- Ranking and average comparison
- Available on **Android**

[Learn more](/speedtest/) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hnimrod.speedtest)

---

### Simple Translator

Translate text into 10 languages with text-to-speech support.

- Support for 10 languages
- Text-to-speech functionality
- Simple and intuitive interface
- Available on **Android**

[Learn more](/translator/) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hnimrod.translator)

---

### Simple Stopwatch

A simple stopwatch with 0.001 second precision. Can also be used as a countdown timer.

- Millisecond precision
- Countdown timer mode
- Available on **Android**

[Learn more](/stopwatch/) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.bj_taro.simpletimer)
