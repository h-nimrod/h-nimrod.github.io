Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Dripo app for iPhone and iPad (the "App"), provided by nimrod project (the "Service Provider") free of charge as an ad-supported service. It explains what information is handled when you use the App, why, how long it is kept, and how you can control or delete it.

Information stored only on your device

Your brew history (date, recipe, number of cups, grind size, brewing time, rating and memo), the coffee beans you choose and your settings (sound, voice guide, vibration) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the Service Provider. They are included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup) according to your iOS settings.

The App uses local notifications to signal each brewing step while it is in the background. Notifications are scheduled on your device; no notification server is used.

Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following services provided by Google. They may collect information automatically when you use the App.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows ads. It may collect your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and operating system information, ad interaction data (such as ad views and taps), diagnostic and performance data, and device identifiers. The identifier for advertisers (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking (see "Tracking and your choices" below); otherwise ads are shown without it. This information is used to show and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud.
  • Google User Messaging Platform asks for your consent to the use of your data for ads in regions where this is required by law (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Your choice is stored on your device.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects how the App is used: screens viewed and actions such as starting or finishing a brew, the recipe, number of cups and grind size you chose, the rating you gave, and whether a memo was entered (the text of your memos and bean names is never sent). It also collects an app instance identifier, the identifier for vendor (IDFV), device model, operating system and app version, and your country or region (estimated from your IP address). If you allow tracking, it may also receive the IDFA. This information is used to understand and improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports: the state of the App when it crashed, device model, operating system and app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. This information is used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads settings for the App (for example, whether ads are shown) using a Firebase installation identifier.

The Service Provider does not sell your information and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact information in the App. Privacy policies of these services:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob
  • Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, Remote Config)
Tracking and your choices

The App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before the IDFA is used to show ads that are more relevant to you. The App works the same and stays free whether or not you allow it.

  • Allow or stop tracking at any time: open the iOS Settings app and go to Privacy & Security > Tracking, and turn Dripo on or off.
  • Change or withdraw your consent for ads (in regions where consent is required): open the App, go to Settings, and tap "Ad Privacy Settings".
  • Stop all collection: delete the App from your device.
Data retention
  • Information stored on your device is kept until you delete it (brew history entries can be deleted in the App's History screen) or delete the App. Copies in your device backups follow your backup settings.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: user-level and event-level data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Google AdMob: information is kept according to Google's data retention policy.
Deleting your data

To delete the information stored on your device, delete brew history entries in the App or delete the App. Information collected by the services above is not linked to your name or contact details, so the Service Provider usually cannot identify it; it is deleted automatically after the retention periods above. If you would like the Service Provider to delete data it may be able to identify, or if you have any other request about your data, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take the necessary action.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.
